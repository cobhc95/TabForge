# TabForge 0.9.4

Stable release. It adds the Composer drum grooves with pattern-based accents, browser-style Ctrl+Tab tab switching, and fixes grooves landing in red (short) bars.

## ✨ New since 0.9.3

- **Composer drum grooves:** six starter grooves in the Composer panel (Straight 8ths, Half-time chug, Blast 16ths, Gallop fill, D-beat, Breakbeat). Audition one at the song's tempo, drag it onto a track at the bar to start from, and choose the tab lane (notation written on the track) or the MIDI lane (a clip on a take lane). Timing stays exactly on the 16th grid; a fixed accent pattern voices the hits in dynamics (mp/mf/f/ff) — quarters strongest, 16th off-beats as ghosts, the snare backbeats and bar-opening kick on top, and the last bar of a multi-bar groove lifted into the repeat.
- **Ctrl+Tab tab switching:** Ctrl+Tab shows the next tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab the previous one, wrapping at both ends, as in a browser. Both are bindable commands (Next tab / Previous tab).

## 🛠️ Fixes

- **Grooves in red bars:** a groove dropped on the tab lane no longer leaves its bars short (drawn red). A hit now lasts until the next hit, so every bar adds up to its time signature.

Windows installers are not code-signed. Linux has not been validated on real hardware and remains experimental. Some JUCE 8 plug-in editors can render black under Wine; eight bridged plug-ins may need 1,024-frame buffers. A faulty native 64-bit VST2 plug-in can still crash the Windows audio engine.
