# TabForge 0.9.4

Stable release. It adds a built-in Reverb, a 101-groove drums-only Composer catalogue with fixed pattern accents, browser-style Ctrl+Tab tab switching, larger score headers, a wider drag gutter in the FX windows, and Band/Compact/Keyboard toggles in the left strip; grooves no longer land in red (short) bars.

## ✨ New since 0.9.3

- **Composer drum grooves (101):** across Rock/Pop, Metal, Punk/Hardcore and Funk/Breakbeat — including double-bass, blast-beat, ballad, half-time, intro and fill grooves. Audition one at the song's tempo, drag it onto a track at the bar to start from, and choose the tab lane (notation written on the track) or the MIDI lane (a clip on a take lane). Grooves may use any General MIDI drum piece (kick, snare, hats, ride, crash, toms…). Timing stays exactly on the 16th grid; a fixed accent pattern voices the hits in dynamics (mp/mf/f/ff) — quarters strongest, 16th off-beats as ghosts, the snare backbeats and bar-opening kick on top, and the last bar of a multi-bar groove lifted into the repeat.
- **Built-in Reverb:** a convolution-based room generated by TabForge — no external IR file required. Size changes the room length; Predelay, Mix, High cut, Low cut, Width and Bypass shape the tail. Add it from the Add plug-in window like any other effect, with a full editor that saves and restores with presets and songs.
- **Composer panel:** drums-only. The instrument cards and the “coming soon” placeholders are gone, and the groove catalogue is visible as soon as the panel opens, with its search filtering grooves and genres.
- **Left strip view toggles:** Band view, Compact mode and Keyboard mode are now default synced toggle buttons in the left strip, and their top-right toolbar icons are gone. View > Band view and View > Compact mode remain, and Band view is bound to **Ctrl+Shift+B** by default.
- **FX chain windows:** the drag handle between the plug-in list and the editor lights on hover and shows the resize cursor; the whole gutter between them is draggable.
- **Score headers:** the authors and the tuning block are larger and easier to read.
- **Ctrl+Tab tab switching:** Ctrl+Tab shows the next tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab the previous one, wrapping at both ends, as in a browser. Both are bindable commands (Next tab / Previous tab).

## 🛠️ Fixes

- **Grooves in red bars:** a groove dropped on the tab lane no longer leaves its bars short (drawn red). A hit now lasts until the next hit, so every bar adds up to its time signature.

Windows installers are not code-signed. Linux has not been validated on real hardware and remains experimental. Some JUCE 8 plug-in editors can render black under Wine; eight bridged plug-ins may need 1,024-frame buffers. A faulty native 64-bit VST2 plug-in can still crash the Windows audio engine.
