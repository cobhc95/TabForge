# TabForge 0.9.6

Stable release. The Mix Table now shows every change at a beat, bars with changes get a red button on the timeline, Focus track stays on through jumps and Mix Table volume changes, and the code was tidied underneath.

## 🎸 New since 0.9.5

- **Red button under the bar number:** the timeline marks every bar where any track changes sound, effects or tempo. It lightens under the pointer, presses in when clicked, and opens the Mix Table on the bar's first changed beat.
- **Composer close button:** an X at the top right of the Composer panel does the same as turning the Composer off.
- **Focus track shortcut:** Ctrl+Alt+F turns Focus track on or off, also while the mixer or a plug-in window is in front. Change it in Settings > Shortcuts.

## 🔧 Changed

- **Mix Table shows every change:** F10 shows the tempo in force at the beat, including a gradual tempo change with its length in the Transition box, and changes made from any other track (a mix point set for all tracks). Setting a tempo writes it for every track.
- **F10 in a marked bar:** on a beat with no change, it opens the Mix Table on the bar's first changed beat instead of an empty table.
- **Tempo slider:** the tempo popup slider covers the song range, 20 to 400 BPM, instead of 30 to 300.
- **Tidier code, same behaviour:** dead code and stale test notes removed; tempo limits, setting ranges, command ids, MIDI numbers and the "all notes off" reset each have one shared definition; six duplicates merged; failures in background actions are logged or shown on the status line instead of lost; menus, scale pick, undo memory, selection, recording and clip-edit clean-up each have one clear owner; new tests pin the compiled MIDI, undo order, selection commands and the main window's commands.

## 🛠️ Fixes

- **Focus track part-way through a song:** after playback reached a volume point written in the Mix Table, the tracks it set went back to their normal level and Focus track stopped working. Volume points are now scaled like every other level.
- **Focus track after a jump:** clicking the timeline or ruler, jumping to a bar or section, a loop restarting after an edit, or an edit while playing could bring every track back to normal until Focus track was turned off and on again. A track added while playing was not lowered either. Focus track now applies to every level as it is sent.
- **Gradual tempo in the Mix Table:** a gradual tempo change (such as bar 101 of Ashen Meridian) was missing from the table.
- **Paste after section:** Paste after section and Paste section on a gap now paste into the track selected in the song, not the track from an earlier menu.
- **Let-ring time:** the 400 to 6000 ms setting now works across its whole range (below 600 ms it did nothing). Values of 600 ms and more sound as before; Guitar Pro files are unaffected.

Windows installers are not code-signed. Linux has not been validated on real hardware and remains experimental.
