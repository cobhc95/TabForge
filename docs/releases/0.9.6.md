# TabForge 0.9.6

Stable release. The Mix Table now shows every change at a beat, bars with changes are marked on the timeline, and Focus track keeps working after a Mix Table volume change.

## 🎸 New since 0.9.5

- **Mix Table shows every change:** F10 shows the tempo in force at the beat, including a gradual tempo change with its length in the Transition box, and changes made from any other track at that beat.
- **Red mark under the bar number:** the timeline marks every bar where any track changes sound, effects or tempo.
- **Focus track shortcut:** Ctrl+Alt+F turns Focus track on or off, also while the mixer or a plug-in window is in front. Change it in Settings > Shortcuts.

## 🛠️ Fixes

- **Paste after section:** pastes into the track selected in the song, not the track from an earlier menu.
- **Let-ring time:** the whole 400 to 6000 ms setting now works (below 600 ms it did nothing). Values of 600 ms and more sound as before.
- **Tempo slider:** the tempo popup slider covers 20 to 400 BPM.
- **Tidier code:** a large clean-up of dead code, duplicates and shared limits, with no change to what you see.

- **Focus track part-way through a song:** after playback reached a volume point written in the Mix Table, the tracks it set went back to their normal level and Focus track stopped working. Volume points are now scaled like every other level.
- **Focus track after a jump:** clicking the timeline, jumping to a bar or section, a loop after an edit, or an edit while playing could bring every track back to its normal level until Focus track was turned off and on again. It now stays on through all of them, and a track added while playing is lowered too.
- **Gradual tempo in the Mix Table:** a gradual tempo change (such as bar 101 of Ashen Meridian) was missing from the table.

Windows installers are not code-signed. Linux has not been validated on real hardware and remains experimental.
