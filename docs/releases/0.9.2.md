# TabForge 0.9.2

Stable release. It reworks the fretboard legend into tappable colour tiles matched to the board height, makes the fretboard look-ahead count notes and preview live while paused or stopped, adds a section-stretch option that turns the added bars into a section of its own, and opens Preferences with Ctrl+, in place of F12.

## 🔎 Fretboard legend

- **Now, Next and Upcoming tiles:** the three small rows are now three side-by-side tiles, each tinted and edged in its own colour with its dot over its name. A click on a tile still opens the colour picker below it, and it shades under the pointer. The legend card is wider to fit them. Scales and Modify are two tiles of the same height and corners in one row under them, with the icon over the label, and their edges line up with the colour tiles.
- **Exact height:** the legend is always exactly as tall as the fretboard. On a short board the whole legend (tiles, text, Scales and Modify) shrinks with it, down to 45% of its normal size; on a tall board the colour tiles and the Scales and Modify tiles grow taller to fill the card, so there is no empty space above or below it. The card is roomier: more padding round its edge, a taller title row and wider gaps between the tiles. It matches the whole neck, binding included, and there is more room between the fretboard and the legend card.
- **Minimise:** a small chevron beside the title minimises the legend to one round button at its corner; the button brings it back. The button shades with an edge under the pointer, its clickable area reaches well beyond the drawn button, and the choice is saved with your settings. While minimised, Scales and Modify stay available from the Tools menu and the right-click menu.

## ⚡ Fretboard look-ahead

- **Counted in notes:** "Next notes shown ahead" now counts notes, not movements — the next movement is always shown whole and later notes fill up to that many in total. So at 10 you see ten notes ahead, chords included.
- **Live preview:** the look-ahead shows while paused and stopped too, at the playhead, so the slider auditions live without pressing play. Apply confirms and Cancel restores the previous settings.

## ⚙️ Fretboard settings

- **More settings and Reset all:** the footer link reads *More settings*, and a *Reset all* button beside it restores every fretboard setting to its default (the Look page to the shown theme's). Cancel still returns to how they were.

## 🧩 Section stretch

- **Add a new section:** dragging a section's start back past the previous section has a new option, *Add a new section in those bars* — now the second choice, under *Add empty bars*, with the rest keeping their order after it. The added bars become a section of their own with a name and colour you set inside the option, while the stretched section keeps its length.

## ⌨️ Shortcuts

- **Preferences with Ctrl+,:** Preferences now opens with Ctrl+, (the comma key). F12 keeps working as a second key, the TuxGuitar preset keeps F7, and custom bindings are untouched.

Windows installers are not code-signed. Linux has not been validated on real hardware and remains experimental. Some JUCE 8 plug-in editors can render black under Wine; eight bridged plug-ins may need 1,024-frame buffers. A faulty native 64-bit VST2 plug-in can still crash the Windows audio engine.
