# Practising

A hard passage rarely gets easier by playing the whole song again and again. It gets easier when you play that one stretch slowly, in time, over and over. TabForge has a tool for each of those habits: loop, speed and click.

By the end of this chapter you can repeat any part of a song, slow it down without changing its pitch, and play along with a metronome and a count-in.

## What you will learn

- Loop a stretch of bars.
- Slow a song down and speed it up again.
- Switch the metronome on and off.
- Use the count-in before playback starts.

## Before you start

Open the demo song with **File > Open…** (`Ctrl+O`). The practice buttons sit with the other transport buttons at the bottom of the window. Their names are **Count-in**, **Metronome** and **Loop**. Hover any of them to read its name.

## Slow down

The **Speed** box changes only how fast the song plays back. The notes keep their pitch, and the written tempo stays as it is. You find the **Speed** box in the top toolbar, left of the tempo box.

Open the list and choose a preset, or type a percentage between 25% and 200%. At 100% the song plays at its written tempo, and 50% plays it at half speed. Typing `75` means 75% speed. It never means 75 beats per minute.

The **BPM** box in the toolbar is different. It holds the song's written tempo, and it is saved with the song. Use **Speed** when you want to practise slowly, and use **BPM** when you want to change the music itself.

> **Tip:** Slow down until you can play the stretch cleanly, then raise the speed a little at a time. Playing fast and sloppy teaches your fingers the mistakes.

## Loop a section

A loop repeats part of the song until you stop it. The **Loop** button decides whether looping is on. What it repeats depends on what you select.

- With nothing selected, the whole song loops.
- With bars selected, only those bars loop.
- A section on the timeline can be looped from its right-click menu.

To select bars, drag sideways across them in the score or on the timeline. A short, deliberate drag is needed, because a tiny wobble counts as a click. Then click the **Loop** button. Hover it to see its shortcut. The status bar says which bars will repeat.

Right-click the **Loop** button to open **Loop settings**. There you can choose how many times to repeat before stopping, and ask for a count-in before every pass. There is also a speed trainer that raises the speed a little on every pass.

> **Note:** Pressing `Esc` clears the selected bars, but it leaves **Loop** on. The whole song then loops. Switch **Loop** off as well if you want the song to play through once.

## Play along with a click

The metronome plays a click on every beat while the song plays. The first beat of each bar is louder, so you can hear where each bar starts. Click the **Metronome** button to switch it on or off. The change takes effect at once, even while the song is playing.

Right-click the **Metronome** button to open **Metronome settings**. You can change the volume, the sound of the click and how finely the beats are divided. A finer division helps with fast or tricky rhythms.

## Use the count-in

A count-in plays a few clicks before the song starts, so your hands are ready. It is very useful with a loop, because you hear the tempo before the first note.

Click the **Count-in** button to switch it on. Right-click it to open **Count-in settings**, where you can set how many bars it counts and when it counts: only at the start, before every section or before every loop pass.

> **Note:** If you switch the count-in on or off while the song is playing, the change starts at the next play. The metronome changes at once.

### Try it: Loop two bars slowly

*Goal: repeat two bars at 75% speed with a click and a count-in.*

1. Open the demo song and click a bar in the score. Then drag across two bars so that both are selected.
2. In the top toolbar, type `75` in the **Speed** box and press `Enter`.
3. Click the **Metronome** button and the **Count-in** button so that both light up.
4. Click the **Loop** button. The status bar names the bars it will repeat.
5. Press `Space`. You hear the count-in, and then the two bars play over and over at three quarters of their speed.
6. Press `Space` to pause. Click **Loop** again to switch it off, and set **Speed** back to `100`.

You know it worked when the same two bars repeated with a click underneath them.

**Stuck?** If you hear nothing at all, see **Chapter 6: Troubleshooting and help**.

## Quick recap

- **Speed** changes how fast you hear the song. **BPM** changes the written tempo.
- **Loop** repeats the selected bars, or the whole song when nothing is selected.
- Right-click **Loop**, **Metronome** or **Count-in** to open their settings.
- The metronome changes at once, and the count-in starts at the next play.
- Press `Space` to start and pause.
