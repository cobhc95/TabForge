# Getting started

This chapter gets you from an empty window to a song playing. You open the demo song, press play, move around the score and change its size on screen. Everything later in the guide builds on these few moves.

## What you will learn

- Open a song with **File > Open…**.
- Play, pause and stop, and go back to the start.
- Move around the score and place the edit cursor.
- Zoom the score in and out.
- Start a new song or open a second one in its own tab.

## Start TabForge

Start TabForge from the Start menu, or run `TabForge.exe`. There is no welcome screen. You land in an empty song called **Untitled**, with one guitar track and some empty bars.

Each song you have open lives in its own document tab in the title bar. Click a tab to switch to that song. A dot on a tab means the song has changes that are not saved yet.

## Open a song

Choose **File > Open…** (`Ctrl+O`). Go to the `Samples` folder beside `TabForge.exe`, choose `TabForge Demo - Ashen Meridian.gp` and click **Open**. This guide calls it Ashen Meridian.

The song opens in the current tab and replaces the empty song. To keep your current song as well, choose **File > Open in new tab…** instead. You can also choose **File > New** for a blank song.

TabForge opens `.tforge`, `.gp`, `.gpx`, `.gp5`, `.gp4` and `.gp3` files. Opening a file never changes it on disk. The file changes only if you save over it, so you can explore freely.

> **Note:** Large songs open in the background. The window stays usable, and the status bar at the bottom shows that the song is loading.

## Play and pause

The transport buttons control playback. They sit in the timeline area at the bottom of the window. Hover a button to read its name, such as **Play / pause**, **Stop** or **Rewind to beginning**.

Press `Space` to start playing from the edit cursor. Press `Space` again to pause. Click **Stop** to stop and return to where you started. **Rewind to beginning** jumps back to the first bar. The same commands are in the **Sound** menu.

While the song plays, the score scrolls to follow it, and the fretboard above the score shows which notes are sounding. The fretboard is a picture of the instrument's neck. For a drum track it turns into a drum map, and for keys it turns into a keyboard.

> **Tip:** If you hear nothing, do not worry yet. **Chapter 6: Troubleshooting and help** has a short checklist.

## Move around the score

The score is the large area in the middle. It shows the selected track as TAB and as standard notation. Click a beat to place the edit cursor there. Clicking only moves the cursor. It never adds a note.

- Roll the mouse wheel over the score to scroll it.
- Press the arrow keys to move the cursor: `Left` and `Right` move one beat, and `Up` and `Down` move between strings.
- Hold `Ctrl` with `Left` or `Right` to move a whole bar at a time.
- Click a bar on the timeline at the bottom to jump to that part of the song.

A bar is one stretch of music between two vertical lines, and bar numbers are written above the score. Playback starts from the edit cursor, so click where you want to begin and then press `Space`.

The status bar along the bottom shows where you are: the bar number, the track name and the current note length.

## Zoom the score

If the notes look too small or too large, change the zoom. The top toolbar has a zoom box with presets, including **Fit width**, and you can type your own value. The zoom buttons beside it step the size up and down. Hover them to see their shortcuts.

Zoom changes only how big the score looks. It never changes the song.

> **Note:** If a panel is missing, choose **View > Reset all panels to default positions**. It puts every panel back and never touches your song.

### Try it: Play the demo song

*Goal: open Ashen Meridian, play a few bars and move the cursor.*

1. Press `Ctrl+O`. The **Open** window appears.
2. Go to the `Samples` folder beside `TabForge.exe`, choose `TabForge Demo - Ashen Meridian.gp` and click **Open**. The song title appears on the document tab.
3. Press `Space`. The song plays and the score follows it.
4. Press `Space` again to pause.
5. Click a beat further along the score, then press `Space`. Playback starts from that beat.
6. Press `Space` to pause, then click the **Rewind to beginning** button.
7. Pick another zoom value in the top toolbar. The score changes size.

You know it worked when the song played from the place you clicked. Close the tab when you finish. Nothing needs saving.

## Quick recap

- **File > Open…** (`Ctrl+O`) opens a song, and **File > Open in new tab…** keeps your current one.
- `Space` plays and pauses. **Stop** and **Rewind to beginning** are on the transport.
- Click a beat to place the edit cursor, and use the arrow keys to move it.
- The zoom box in the top toolbar changes the size of the score.
- Hover any control to see its name and its shortcut.
